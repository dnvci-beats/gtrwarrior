# Smoke on the Water - Riff

## Description

The main riff from "Smoke on the Water" by Deep Purple is arguably the most recognizable guitar riff in the history of rock music. It's often one of the first riffs a beginner guitarist learns. It was played by Ritchie Blackmore on a Fender Stratocaster.

## Content

The riff is famously played using two-note chords, which are a type of double-stop. The original recording features Ritchie Blackmore plucking the strings with his fingers (not a pick) to get a softer, rounder tone. The chords are all parallel fourths.

The riff is composed of three main parts.
