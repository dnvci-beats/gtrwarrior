# Blackbird - The Beatles

## Description

"Blackbird" is a classic acoustic song by The Beatles from the 1968 "White Album". It was written by Paul McCartney and features a beautiful and unique fingerstyle guitar part. It's a great piece for any aspiring fingerstyle guitarist to learn.

## Content

### The Technique

The song is played in a style that is a variation of fingerstyle. Paul McCartney uses his thumb to play a constant bass note, while his index finger plays melody and harmony notes on the higher strings. A key part of the sound is the use of open strings, particularly the open G string, which rings out through many of the chord shapes.

The rhythm is syncopated, and the time signature is primarily 3/4, which gives it a gentle, waltz-like feel.

### The Chords and Shapes

The song is built around a series of two-note and three-note shapes, primarily on the A, G, and B strings. The genius of the song is how the bassline moves up and down the neck while the melody is played on top.

**Intro/Verse Shape:**
The song opens with a G chord shape, but it's played in a specific way.

-   Your index finger is on the 3rd fret of the A string (C note).
-   Your middle finger is on the 4th fret of the G string (B note).
-   The open B string is played.

This creates a G/B chord voicing. The thumb plucks the A string and the B string simultaneously, followed by the index finger plucking the G string.
