# Sweet Child o' Mine - Guns N' Roses

## Description

The opening riff to "Sweet Child o' Mine" by Guns N' Roses is one of the most famous and instantly recognizable guitar riffs in rock music. It was written by guitarist Slash and has a unique, melodic, and looping quality.

## Content

### The Sound & Tuning

The riff was originally played by Slash on a Les Paul guitar through a Marshall amplifier. The key to the sound is the neck pickup, which gives it a warm, rounded, and "singing" tone, with a good amount of overdrive but not heavy distortion. The guitars on the recording are tuned down a half-step (Eb Ab Db Gb Bb Eb). This means that while the riff is played using D, C, and G shapes, the actual key of the song is Db, Cb, and Gb.

### The Riff's Structure

The riff is a repeating eight-note pattern that is moved over three different chords: D, C, and G. It's an exercise in string skipping and has a very melodic, almost nursery-rhyme-like quality.

The pattern is based on arpeggiating the underlying chord across the G, B, and high E strings. The exact pattern is a subject of much debate and there are many ways to play it, but the general idea is a looping, syncopated melody.
