# Stairway to Heaven - Chords

## Description

"Stairway to Heaven" by Led Zeppelin is one of the most famous rock songs of all time. The introduction is a beautiful and iconic piece of fingerstyle guitar. This document provides a simplified overview of the chord progression for the intro.

## Content

The intro to "Stairway to Heaven" is based on a descending bassline over a repeating arpeggiated pattern. The chords are played with a fingerpicking pattern. The basic chord progression is as follows:

**Am - G#+ - C/G - D/F# - Fmaj7 - G - Am**

Let's break down these chords:

-   **Am:** A minor (A C E)
-   **G#+ (G# augmented):** This is often played as a passing chord. It's more accurately a G# bass note under the Am shape, creating an Am/G#. The augmented sound comes from the relationship between G# and the E in the arpeggio. A simpler way to think of it is just changing the bass note from A to G#.
-   **C/G:** A C major chord with a G in the bass.
-   **D/F#:** A D major chord with an F# in the bass.
-   **Fmaj7:** An F major 7th chord (F A C E).
-   **G:** A G major chord.
-   **Am:** Returning to A minor.
