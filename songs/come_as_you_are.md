# Come As You Are - Nirvana

## Description

"Come As You Are" is one of Nirvana's most popular songs, from their seminal 1991 album "Nevermind". The main guitar riff, played by Kurt Cobain, is instantly recognizable for its watery, chorus-drenched tone and chromatic, hypnotic melody.

## Content

### The Sound and Tuning

The signature sound of the riff comes from an Electro-Harmonix Small Clone chorus pedal. The guitar is tuned down a full step from standard tuning (D-G-C-F-A-D). This gives the song a heavier, darker feel.

The tab below is written as if the guitar were in standard tuning, so the fret numbers are relative to standard. If you are playing in standard tuning, it will sound a step higher than the record, but the pattern is the same.

### The Main Riff

The riff is a simple, repeating pattern played on the two lowest strings. It's a great exercise in alternate picking and keeping a steady rhythm.

```
e|---------------------------------|---------------------------------|
B|---------------------------------|---------------------------------|
G|---------------------------------|---------------------------------|
D|---------------------------------|---------------------------------|
A|-----------0---0-----------2---2-|-----------0---0-----------2---2-|
E|-0-0-1-2-----2---2-2-1-0-----0---|-0-0-1-2-----2---2-2-1-0-----0---|
```

Let's break it down:
-   The riff is built around the open low E string (which sounds as a D on the record).
-   It uses a chromatic walk-up (0-1-2) and walk-down (2-1-0) on the low E string.
-   It also uses the open A string and the 2nd fret of the A string (B).
-   The entire riff just repeats throughout the verses of the song.

### The Chorus

The chorus of the song moves to a heavier, distorted sound and plays a two-chord progression:

**F#m - A**
