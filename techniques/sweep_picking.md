# Sweep Picking

## Description

Sweep picking is an advanced guitar technique used to play arpeggios and other note patterns at high speeds. It involves using a continuous "sweeping" motion of the pick across multiple strings, rather than a separate pick stroke for each note. It is most commonly associated with shred and neoclassical metal guitar playing.

## Content

### The Core Motion

The key to sweep picking is economy of motion.

-   **Descending Sweep:** When moving from a higher string to a lower string (e.g., from the high E string to the G string), you use a single, continuous downstroke. The pick "sweeps" across the strings.
-   **Ascending Sweep:** When moving from a lower string to a higher string, you use a single, continuous upstroke.

### Synchronization is Key

The biggest challenge in sweep picking is synchronizing the fretting hand with the picking hand. Each note must be fretted at the precise moment the pick strikes the string, and then immediately released to avoid notes ringing into each other. This is crucial for achieving a clean, articulate sound. If notes bleed into each other, the result is a sloppy, undefined sound.

### How to Practice

1.  **Start with Two Strings:** Begin with a simple two-string arpeggio. For example, play the 12th fret on the B string and the 12th fret on the high E string. Use a downstroke to sweep across both, then practice the reverse with an upstroke.
2.  **Use a Metronome:** Practice very slowly with a metronome to build synchronization. Speed is a byproduct of accuracy.
3.  **Muting:** Muting is essential to keep the notes separate and clean.
    -   **Palm Muting:** The side of your picking hand can rest on the strings you are not playing to keep them quiet.
    -   **Fretting-Hand Muting:** Use the undersides of your fretting fingers to mute adjacent strings.
4.  **Build Up to More Strings:** Gradually add more strings to your arpeggio shapes. Three-string and five-string arpeggios are very common.

### Example Shape (A minor arpeggio)

Here is a common 3-string A minor arpeggio shape:

```
      (down) (down) (down) (up)  (up)  (up)

B|---------13----------13-------------------|
G|------14----------------14----------------|
D|---14----------------------14-------------|

```
