# Fingerstyle Guitar Basics

## Description

Fingerstyle (also known as fingerpicking) is a technique of playing the guitar by plucking the strings directly with the fingertips, fingernails, or picks attached to fingers, as opposed to using a single flatpick. It allows the player to perform multiple musical parts at once, such as a bassline, chords, and a melody simultaneously.

## Content

### Hand Position and Finger Names

In classical and fingerstyle notation, the fingers of the picking hand are given names from Spanish:

-   **p** = *pulgar* (thumb)
-   **i** = *índice* (index)
-   **m** = *medio* (middle)
-   **a** = *anular* (ring)
-   **c** = *chiquito* (pinky - rarely used)

**Basic Hand Position:**
-   Your wrist should be relatively straight, not overly bent.
-   Your thumb (**p**) typically plays the bass strings (E, A, D). It strikes the string with a downward motion.
-   Your index (**i**), middle (**m**), and ring (**a**) fingers are assigned to the higher strings. A common starting point is:
    -   **i** on the G string
    -   **m** on the B string
    -   **a** on the high E string
-   These fingers pluck the string with an upward motion, curling into your palm.

### Basic Patterns (Arpeggios)

The foundation of fingerstyle is playing arpeggios, which is simply playing the notes of a chord one at a time.

**Example 1: A simple pattern on a C Major chord.**

Hold a C Major chord.
1.  The thumb (**p**) plays the root note on the A string.
2.  The index finger (**i**) plays the G string.
3.  The middle finger (**m**) plays the B string.
4.  The ring finger (**a**) plays the high E string.

A very common pattern is **P-I-M-A-M-I**.
```
   C Major
e|---------0-------|
B|-------1---1-----|
G|-----0-------0---|
D|-----------------|
A|---3-------------|
E|-----------------|
      p a m i m i
```

### Travis Picking

A very popular style of fingerstyle is "Travis picking," named after Merle Travis. It's characterized by a steady, alternating bassline played by the thumb while the fingers play syncopated melodies on the higher strings.

**Basic Travis Picking Pattern (on a C Major chord):**
The thumb (**p**) alternates between two bass notes, usually the root and the fifth of the chord, on the beat. The index (**i**) and middle (**m**) fingers play melody notes on the off-beats.

```
   C Major
      1  &  2  &  3  &  4  &
e|--------------------------|
B|------1-----1-----1-----1-|
G|---------0-----------0----|
D|---2-----------2----------|
A|3-------------------------|
E|--------------------------|
      p  i  p  i  p  i  p  i
```
(This is a simplified example. The thumb would alternate between different bass notes).

## Resources

-   Fingerstyle is a deep and rewarding way to play the guitar. Start with simple arpeggio patterns and focus on developing a consistent, even tone with each finger. Listen to fingerstyle masters like Chet Atkins, Merle Travis, Tommy Emmanuel, and Sungha Jung for inspiration.
