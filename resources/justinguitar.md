# JustinGuitar.com

## Description

JustinGuitar.com is one of the most popular and respected websites for learning guitar online. It is run by Australian guitarist Justin Sandercoe. The website offers a vast collection of free guitar lessons for all skill levels, from absolute beginners to advanced players.

## Content

### Key Features

-   **Structured Courses:** The website is famous for its structured lesson plans, such as the Beginner's Course, which takes students from holding the guitar for the first time to playing songs.
-   **Comprehensive Content:** Justin covers a huge range of topics, including:
    -   Basic chords and scales
    -   Music theory
    -   Ear training
    -   Technique exercises
    -   Song tutorials for hundreds of popular songs
    -   Style-specific lessons (blues, rock, folk, etc.)
-   **Free Lessons:** The vast majority of the lesson content is available for free, which has made it an invaluable resource for millions of students worldwide.
-   **Community:** The site has a large and active community forum where students can ask questions and interact with each other.

### Why it's a great resource

Justin's teaching style is clear, friendly, and encouraging. He breaks down complex topics into small, manageable steps. The structured nature of his courses provides a clear path for progression, which can be very helpful for self-taught guitarists who might not know what to learn next.
