# Guitar World

## Description

Guitar World is one of the oldest and most well-known guitar magazines in the world. It has been in publication since 1980. Like Premier Guitar, it exists as a print magazine, a website, and a YouTube channel. It is traditionally known for its focus on rock, metal, and blues guitar.

## Content

### Key Offerings

1.  **Magazine and Website:**
    -   **Artist Interviews:** Guitar World is famous for its interviews with rock and metal guitar icons.
    -   **Lessons and Columns:** The magazine has a long history of featuring lessons from famous guitarists. Many well-known players have had columns in the magazine over the years.
    -   **Transcriptions:** The magazine is known for its accurate transcriptions of popular rock and metal songs.
    -   **Gear Reviews:** Reviews of new guitars, amps, and pedals, often with a focus on rock and metal gear.

2.  **YouTube Channel:**
    -   Features video lessons, gear demos, and interviews.
    -   Often posts content related to the articles in the print magazine.

### Why it's a great resource

Guitar World has a long and storied history in the guitar community. It's a fantastic resource for players who are interested in the history and techniques of rock and metal guitar. The transcriptions have always been a major draw, providing a way for players to accurately learn the songs of their heroes. While its focus is somewhat narrower than some other media outlets, it is an authoritative voice in that space.
