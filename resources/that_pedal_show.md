# That Pedal Show

## Description

That Pedal Show is a very popular YouTube channel dedicated to guitar effects pedals and tone-crafting. It is hosted by two friends, Daniel Steinhardt and Mick Taylor, who have extensive experience in the music industry (Daniel runs a high-end pedalboard building company, and Mick is a professional guitarist and journalist).

## Content

### The Format

The show's format is typically a long-form discussion and demonstration. Daniel and Mick will take a topic (e.g., "What is a Klon Centaur?", "How to use a compressor", "Overdrive vs. Distortion") and spend an hour or more exploring it in great depth. They use a wide variety of high-quality guitars, amps, and pedals, and they A/B test everything meticulously.

### Key Features

-   **In-Depth Knowledge:** The hosts are extremely knowledgeable and passionate about guitar gear. They go beyond surface-level reviews and get into the history, science, and practical application of the gear they are discussing.
-   **Focus on Concepts:** While they demo specific products, the show is more about teaching the *concepts* behind the gear. They want viewers to understand *why* things sound the way they do, so they can make better decisions about their own tone.
-   **High-Quality Production:** The show is very well-produced, with excellent audio and multiple camera angles.
-   **Transparency:** They are very open about their process and any relationships they have with manufacturers.

### Why it's a great resource

For anyone who is serious about learning how guitar effects work and how to combine them to create great tones, That Pedal Show is arguably the best resource on the internet. It's like a free weekly masterclass in guitar tone. The long-form format allows for a level of detail that you won't find in most other gear demo channels.
