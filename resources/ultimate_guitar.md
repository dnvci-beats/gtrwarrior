# Ultimate-Guitar.com

## Description

Ultimate Guitar (UG) is one of the largest online communities for guitarists and musicians. It is most famous for its massive user-generated library of guitar and bass tablature (tabs) and chords for an enormous number of songs.

## Content

### Key Features

-   **Massive Tab Library:** The primary feature of Ultimate Guitar is its collection of millions of tabs and chords for songs from all genres and eras. These are contributed by the user community, so the quality can vary, but there are often many versions of each song to choose from.
-   **Tab Ratings:** Users can rate the accuracy of tabs, which helps to identify the best versions.
-   **Pro Tabs:** UG offers a premium subscription service ("Pro") that provides access to more accurate, officially licensed, and interactive tabs. These often include multiple instrument tracks, looping features, and adjustable playback speed.
-   **Articles and Reviews:** The site also features news articles, interviews with artists, and reviews of new gear.
-   **Community and Forums:** Like other large music sites, UG has a forum for discussion on various topics related to guitar and music.

### Why it's a useful resource

For learning to play specific songs, Ultimate Guitar is often the first place a guitarist will look. The sheer volume of its library means you can find tabs for even obscure songs. While the quality of free tabs can be inconsistent, the rating system helps to filter for the most accurate ones. The Pro service adds a lot of powerful tools for more serious study of a song.
