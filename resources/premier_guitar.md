# Premier Guitar

## Description

Premier Guitar is a major media brand for guitarists and other musicians. It exists as a monthly print magazine, a digital magazine, a popular YouTube channel, and a website with articles, reviews, and lessons. It's known for its in-depth gear reviews, artist interviews, and high-quality journalism.

## Content

### Key Offerings

1.  **YouTube Channel:**
    -   **Rig Rundowns:** This is their most famous series. They go on tour with major bands and have the artists' guitar techs walk them through every single piece of gear used on stage by the band. It's a treasure trove of information for gearheads.
    -   **Gear Demos:** They produce high-quality, no-nonsense demos of new pedals, guitars, and amps.
    -   **Interviews:** In-depth interviews with famous and influential guitarists.

2.  **Magazine (Print and Digital):**
    -   Features long-form articles, deep-dive gear reviews, and columns by respected industry figures.
    -   Often includes transcriptions and lessons.

3.  **Website:**
    -   An archive of their articles and reviews.
    -   Regularly updated with news, new product announcements, and blog-style content.

### Why it's a great resource

Premier Guitar is respected for its professional and serious approach to guitar journalism. Unlike some other resources, it's not just aimed at beginners. It provides content for players of all levels, including serious hobbyists and professional musicians. Their Rig Rundowns are an invaluable and unique resource for anyone interested in how professional touring bands create their sound.
